Creamy White Sauce Pasta

Prep Time 10 min
Cook Time 15 min
Servings 2
Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • Penne Pasta (2 cups)
  • Milk & Heavy Cream
  • Butter & Garlic

Steps

  1. Boil pasta until al dente.
  2. Sauté garlic in butter, add flour, then slowly whisk in milk.
  3. Toss pasta in the sauce and top with cheese.

Chef Tip: Save some pasta water to thin out the sauce if it gets too thick!

Aromatic Chicken Biryani

Prep 30 min
Cook 45 min
Servings 4
Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • Basmati Rice
  • Marinated Chicken
  • Saffron Milk

Steps

  1. Cook rice halfway.
  2. Layer meat and rice in a heavy pot.
  3. Dum cook for 20 minutes.
